This is a fan English patch for *Puyo Puyo Tetris* on 3DS, carrying Sega's own official English text and voice work over from the PC release, DLC included. This page covers what the patch fixes overall, one line of history per build, and what's still known to be off.

## What this build fixes

- **The DLC starts quickly now.** It holds only the parts that carry anything instead of Sega's hundreds of empty slots, so on one console it starts in about 12 seconds instead of about a minute and a half.
- **EX chapter 8 to 10 quotes and clear conditions fit their boxes.** Lines that ran off the edge of their bubble or box break in a different place now, with the same words. The "Suzuran Jr. High 4" name is drawn smaller to fit its plate.
- **Three more Japanese leftovers are English now.** The combo bar on the Tetris side reads "COMBO" (it said "REN"), and the small notice for a chain of line clears reads "CHAIN!" and "LINES". The replay speed hint reads "Press left/right to adjust playback speed!", in Sega's own wording. The sign on the Magic School gate pillars was the Japanese "pu" character in battle backgrounds, cutscenes and the small school scene; it's a P now.
- **Over a hundred pictures were redrawn from Sega's original Japanese art.** They cover the menus (two of them tile previews on the main menu), the Club, the internet, replay and results screens, the "Ready" call, the tournament card, a banner and the DLC chapter strips. The English lettering from the earlier pass had problems, so I went back to the originals and did them again. Plates, pills and icons it had erased are back, the lettering has the original's outline, shading and size, and text in grids is one consistent size.
- **Japanese leftovers on a few screens are English now.** A handful of places the earlier pass missed have been done.
- **Some textures looked slightly washed out or see-through, and they're exact again.** The cause was a 4-bit colour rounding that came in with the earlier fan translation.
- **A mode-name typo is fixed, and a few labels read differently.** "Bing Bang Mode" is now "Big Bang Mode". The wins counter (it said "beaten"), "Chain FX" for the Chain Animations option, "chains+" and "colors+" in a rule tip, "RANK" on the club rank pill, "Interstellar Dream" on a DLC chapter strip (it had an extra "An"), and the tournament card's "Wins", "High Score:" and "Final Score:" (they were "beaten", "Best Record" and "This Run").
- **Some things are still not right.** A few labels are wider than the box the original art had for them, and some of the small grids use condensed lettering to fit. They're in the known problems below.

## Known problems

- DLC 0.3.3 has the same trimmed contents as a test build played through on a console. The 0.3.3 files themselves, the re-broken lines and the Japanese-voice DLC haven't been run yet, one chapter 9 quote needs four lines, and a few long quotes may still touch the edge of their box.
- 1.1.3's three picture fixes were checked by reading the pictures back out of the built files, plus an Azahar run that showed the title screen reading ENG 1.1.3 and a letter on both gate pillars in the Staff Roll. The combo bar and the CHAIN!/LINES notice haven't come up in a match yet, and I haven't seen the replay speed hint or the gate in battles and cutscenes. That run was on the English-voice edition; the Japanese-voice edition hasn't been booted.
- 1.1.2 was checked on an emulator only, not on a real console. In Azahar (English-voice edition) the menus and tiles, Options, Versus setup, My Data, the Adventure mission panel and the "Ready" call all showed English, with no crash, freeze or garbled screen. Not seen yet: "Ready" in a versus match, the DLC chapter strips, the tournament card, the Japanese-voice edition and a console.
- Ten other pictures, mostly small tile previews, still show Japanese. They're listed in DETAILS.md.
- A few labels are wider than the box the original art had for them, and some of the small grids use condensed lettering to fit.
- Four small rank-suffix pictures, the Japanese mark that follows a rank number, are still Japanese on two replay and league screens.
- The right-hand column on the tournament screens is text the game builds itself, and it may read oddly.
- A few dozen strings are Japanese on purpose: the character-entry keyboards (the hiragana/katakana/kanji inventories in name entry) and the developer tables define what can be typed and aren't translations waiting to happen.
- Three DLC story lines stay Japanese (chapter 8 scene 5, chapter 9 scenes 4 and 7); those lines were re-recorded for the 3DS and have no English take.
- Voices can sound a touch less crisp than on PC. Every English voice is re-encoded from Steam's recordings with a home-grown encoder that's a little weaker than Sega's. Pitch and timing are correct.
- Stylised mode names in the online menus (Fusion, Swap, Party, Big Bang) are plain white where the Japanese had a thick two-tone outline.
- The HOLD and NEXT labels on the Tetris side of a mixed match look clipped at the top; that's Sega's own art, and the PC version shows the same clipping.
- Prefecture buttons in the online rankings are tiny: nine-letter names in boxes drawn for two kanji.
- A few merged/rotated strings on the league result screen are Japanese: the vertical "you lose" / "congratulations" art doesn't split cleanly from the artwork around it.
- Decorative screenshot thumbnails and the small "New Record" ornament stay Japanese, left as artwork on purpose.
- A faint Puzzle League medal crest stays Japanese; Sega's own English version of it read worse once fitted here, so the Japanese one was kept.
- The Slot rule's "N more Puyo" counter and a handful of online menu preview cards stay Japanese; Sega's PC release has no matching English art for either.
- Starting a local Multiplayer host and backing out crashed the emulator; this is an emulator-side assertion, not the patch, and is untested on real hardware.

The full list, with what to report and how, is in TESTING.md.

## Files

Install order is the Japanese base game, then the patched base, then the Japanese v1.2.0 update, then the patched DLC.

- **Base game**: `PuyoPuyoTetris-xdelta-patches-1.1.3.zip` (the base and DLC xdelta3 patches together, with a readme carrying every hash; the file romhacking.net links to), or `PuyoPuyoTetris-Base-xdelta.zip` on its own (xdelta3 patch for your own decrypted dump; the only route to the English HOME menu banner), or `PuyoPuyoTetris-LayeredFS.zip` for Luma3DS.
- **DLC**: `PuyoPuyoTetris-DLC-patched.cia`, or `PuyoPuyoTetris-DLC-xdelta.zip` for your own decrypted DLC dump.
- **Japanese-voice edition**: `PuyoPuyoTetris-JP-voices-xdelta-patches-1.1.3.zip`, `PuyoPuyoTetris-JP-voices-LayeredFS.zip` and `PuyoPuyoTetris-DLC-JP-voices-0.3.3.cia`. Rebuilt along with the main release, since the picture redraws aren't voice files; at the same 1.1.3 / DLC 0.3.3 as the English-voice build. Install one edition or the other, not both.

If you're using any of the xdelta zips, dump the title as an encrypted CIA with GodMode9 and decrypt it on the PC; GodMode9's own decrypt gives a file of the right size that the patch will refuse. Sizes and hashes for every file are in the README.

The full technical history, including every measurement behind these updates, is kept in BUILD_NOTES.md.
